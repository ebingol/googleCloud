# OQ-02 — Choreography versus orchestration

5 soru. Her soruda seçilecek cevap sayısı belirtilmiştir.

**Kaynak türü:** Paylaştığın Choreography and Orchestration quizi ve belirtilen resmî dokümanlar. Bu konular mevcut Introduction to Microservices PDF’sinde anlatılmıyor.

## 1. Which two choreography benefits were accepted in your quiz?

**Select two.**

- A. Loosely coupled services
- B. Decentralized control
- C. The producer maintains full control over every downstream step
- D. The entire business process is always easy to understand

## 2. A producer publishes an event and independent services react. Where does control primarily reside in choreography?

**Select one.**

- A. Only in a mandatory central orchestrator
- B. In one shared application process
- C. At the participating service level
- D. In a single producer that invokes every downstream operation

## 3. Why is full producer control over downstream operations not a choreography benefit?

**Select one.**

- A. All consumers must run in the producer's process
- B. Independent consumers determine their reactions to events
- C. No consumer is allowed to react without a central workflow definition
- D. Every event must contain all consumers' private code

## 4. Which claim should not be inferred from decentralized control?

**Select one.**

- A. Control can live at service level
- B. The complete business process is always easy to follow
- C. Services can be loosely coupled
- D. Services can operate independently

## 5. A team wants a centrally defined sequence coordinating several services. Which pattern best matches?

**Select one.**

- A. A single local retry loop with no process definition
- B. Pure decentralized choreography with no coordinator
- C. Independent event reactions without shared sequencing
- D. Service orchestration
