# U03-08 — Dedicated, Partner and Cross-Cloud Interconnect

5 soru. Soruların her birinde kaç seçenek seçileceği belirtilmiştir.

## 1. Which option supplies direct private connections to Google in the module?

**Select one.**

- A. Cloud VPN
- B. Dedicated Interconnect
- C. Direct Peering
- D. Carrier Peering

## 2. What qualification accompanies the up-to-99.99% SLA claim for Dedicated Interconnect?

**Select one.**

- A. Every single circuit automatically has the maximum SLA
- B. Only the connection name must match a convention
- C. Connections must use topologies meeting Google's specifications
- D. All topologies provide the same SLA without requirements

## 3. A data center cannot reach a Dedicated Interconnect colocation facility. Which supported-provider option fits?

**Select one.**

- A. Cloud VPN as a physical private circuit
- B. Direct Peering without a provider
- C. Partner Interconnect
- D. A Shared VPC service project

## 4. Which option creates dedicated connectivity between Google Cloud and another cloud provider?

**Select one.**

- A. Partner Interconnect to an on-premises site
- B. Carrier Peering
- C. Cross-Cloud Interconnect
- D. Cloud CDN Interconnect

## 5. Which two Cross-Cloud Interconnect connection sizes are stated in the PDF?

**Select two.**

- A. 10 Gbps
- B. 100 Gbps
- C. 100 Mbps
- D. 10 Mbps
