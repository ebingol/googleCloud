# U07-02 — Zero-shot, one-shot, few-shot and role prompts

5 soru. Soruların her birinde kaç seçenek seçileceği belirtilmiştir.

## 1. A prompt asks a question without providing any worked examples. What type does the course call it?

**Select one.**

- A. Fine-tuning
- B. Few-shot
- C. Zero-shot
- D. One-shot

## 2. A prompt includes exactly one example before the new input. What type is it?

**Select one.**

- A. Role prompting alone
- B. Zero-shot
- C. One-shot
- D. Few-shot

## 3. How many examples does the course require at minimum for few-shot prompting?

**Select one.**

- A. Exactly ten
- B. Two
- C. Exactly one
- D. Zero

## 4. What is the function of role prompting in the course?

**Select one.**

- A. Guarantee factual correctness
- B. Assign real cloud permissions automatically
- C. Give the model a frame of reference for answering
- D. Retrain all model weights for the role

- A. Italy: Rome. Japan: Tokyo. France: ___

- C. What is the capital of France?
