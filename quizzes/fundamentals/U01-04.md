- A. ISO 27001
- B. SOC 2
- C. ISO 14001
- D. ISO 9001

## 2. What cooling resource is used by the Hamina, Finland data-center example?

**Select one.**

- A. Only chilled water produced on every server rack
- B. Only conventional air conditioning without seawater
- C. Sea water from the Bay of Finland

- A. Achieving annual renewable-energy matching for the first time
- B. Eliminating the need for any physical servers

- A. Virtualization removes all data-center energy requirements
- B. Cloud services operate without physical servers
- C. Only customer devices consume electricity
